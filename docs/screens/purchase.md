# Purchase

The buying chain: **Purchase order → Goods receipt (GRN) → Purchase bill → Debit note**. Purchase orders and
GRNs are built; bills and debit notes come next. For purchases the place of supply is the factory's own state.

## Purchase orders

**Purchase → Orders.** What's been ordered from suppliers, the **Delivery by** date, and a **Received** column (none,
partly or fully). Draft → Sent (**Mark as sent**) → Cancelled.

## Goods receipts (GRNs)

**Purchase → GRNs.** Goods that came in, with the **supplier's challan no.** Stores staff (operators) record them,
fresh or against a PO. Draft → Received → Cancelled. These screenshots are taken as an operator.

### New GRN from a PO

The PO's pending lines come in. Per line: **Accepted** (goes into stock and off the PO) and **Rejected** (sent
back). Plus the supplier's challan no. and date, vehicle, transporter and e-way bill.
